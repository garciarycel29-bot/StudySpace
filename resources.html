Self-Help Guides

Small, gentle activities you can try any time. Open one that feels right for you.

Deep Breathing

Breathe in slowly through your nose for four counts, hold for four, then breathe out gently for six. Repeat until your shoulders feel softer.

⏱ About 5 minutes
How to Reach Out for Professional Help
  1. Decide what you would like to talk about — it does not have to be perfectly clear.
  2. Choose a campus office from the directory above and send a short message or email.
  3. Pick a time that works for you. Every appointment is private and confidential.
  4. Show up as you are. Listening is the counselor's job, not judging.
Gentle Stretching

Roll your shoulders, reach for the sky, and gently stretch your neck. Moving a little helps release the tension you didn't notice you were holding.

⏱ About 5 minutes
Journaling

Write down whatever is on your mind — no editing, no rules. Seeing your thoughts on paper often makes them feel lighter.

⏱ About 10 minutes
5-4-3-2-1 Grounding

Name five things you can see, four you can touch, three you can hear, two you can smell, and one you can taste. It gently brings you back to the present moment.

⏱ About 5 minutes
Mindful Walk

Take a short walk and notice the sky, the sounds, and the feeling of each step. Let the movement carry some of the weight for a while.

⏱ About 10 minutes
Gratitude Check-In

Think of three small things that went okay today — a kind message, a good meal, a moment of quiet. Noticing the good is a gentle daily habit.

⏱ About 3 minutes
