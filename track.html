Emotional Self-Assessment

Take a moment to understand how you have been feeling lately. Answer honestly. There are no right or wrong answers.

Part I. General Emotional Check-In

Think about how you have been feeling over the past two weeks.

1. How have you been feeling emotionally over the past two weeks?

2. Do you feel that you are emotionally okay with how things are going in your life right now?

Part II. Emotional Assessment

Read each statement and choose how often you have experienced it during the past two weeks.

Answer Guide:

Never – I haven't experienced this.

Some days – I experienced this occasionally.

Most days – I experienced this frequently.

Nearly every day – I experienced this almost daily.

A. Stress and Feeling Overwhelmed

3. I feel overwhelmed by the responsibilities or tasks I need to accomplish.

4. I find it difficult to relax because I keep thinking about the things I need to do.

5. I feel pressured when I have too many things to handle at once.

B. Anxiety and Excessive Worrying

6. I feel nervous or uneasy even when there is no clear reason.

7. I worry about things that might happen in the future.

8. I find it difficult to stop thinking about things that worry me.

C. Sadness and Low Mood

9. I feel sad or down even when I cannot explain why.

10. I have less interest in activities that I usually enjoy.

11. I sometimes feel disconnected from the things and people around me.

D. Emotional Exhaustion

12. I feel mentally tired even after taking a break.

13. I find it difficult to concentrate on my schoolwork or daily responsibilities.

14. I sometimes struggle to find the motivation to complete my usual activities.

E. Social Connection and Loneliness

15. I sometimes feel alone even when I am surrounded by other people.

16. I find it difficult to express what I am feeling to other people.

17. I feel like I do not have someone I can comfortably talk to when I have problems.

Your Emotional Reflection

Remember that your feelings are valid. Take your time, and do not hesitate to reach out to someone you trust when you need support.

This assessment is intended for emotional awareness and self-reflection only. It is not a clinical test and cannot diagnose mental health conditions.
